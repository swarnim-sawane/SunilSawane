01

When the package arrives

  • Photograph the package before opening it if you notice dents, cuts, moisture, or unusual handling marks.
  • Open the package carefully and keep all packing material until you have inspected the artwork.
  • Report transit damage within 24 hours of delivery with photos of the outer package, inner packing, and artwork.

02

Damage support

  • Transit damage is handled through direct collector support, documentation, and courier follow-up where applicable.
  • The aim is to resolve the issue fairly while protecting the original artwork and the collector record.
  • Do not discard the package until the issue has been reviewed.

03

Returns

  • Because every artwork is an original, returns are reviewed case by case.
  • A return cannot be accepted for a change of mind after the artwork has been safely delivered as described.
  • If an order has incorrect buyer or address details, contact immediately before dispatch.

Damage support starts fastest when the first message includes the order number, artwork title, photos, and delivery date.
